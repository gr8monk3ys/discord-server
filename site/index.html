04 — House rules

Short, because they should be

  1. 18+ only. If you're under 18, this isn't the server for you.
  2. Be decent. Trash-talk the play, not the person. No racism, sexism or slurs.
  3. Play fair. No cheats, no exploits, no selling accounts.
  4. No spam or self-promo, and no DM advertising to members.
  5. No NSFW anywhere. Spoilers go under spoiler tags.
  6. Need a squad? Use /lfg and the game pings, not @everyone.
  7. Mods have the final word. Report anything off with /report.

05 — FAQ

Questions

Do I have to be good at games?

Not at all. That's the point. Nobody's grading your K/D here.

What happens when I join?

A quick check to keep bots out, then you pick your games and land in general. Run /quest for a short starter quest that shows you around and pays 500 coins. Say hi.

How do I get roles?

Pick your games during onboarding, then use the button panel in 🎭・roles (or /roles) for platform, region, play time and pings. Level roles, Tournament Champ and Clip of the Week are earned, and colour roles are in the /shop.

How do levels work?

You earn XP for chatting (once a minute, so spamming doesn't help) and for time in voice. Level 5 makes you a Regular, then Veteran at 10, Elite at 20, Legend at 30 and Mythic at 50. Check yours with /rank.

Is voice chat required?

No. Plenty of people just text. Voice is there when you want it, and you can make your own room from the lobby.

What time zone is everyone in?

Scheduled events run on US Pacific time, but people play at all hours.

My game isn't listed. Can I still join?

Yep. Suggest it in the suggestions forum; if enough people play it, it gets its own channel and role.

Can my server partner with yours?

If it's a friendly community that fits the vibe, yes. Join, run /partner apply, and staff will review it. Approved partners are listed in 🤝・partners. Invites are checked weekly, so use a permanent one.
