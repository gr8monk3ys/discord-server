Your first week

Four things a new member ends up doing. None of them is required.

  • Hop in voice

    The Lobby is the open voice room. If someone is in it, join them: that is how most games here start. For a room of your own, join New Squad and Front Desk makes one named after what you're playing.

  • Find people for a game

    Post with /lfg: what you're playing, when, and how many you need. Members who picked that game get the ping. On a server this size, asking in general works too.

  • Get sorted into a house

    Front Desk, the server's bot, puts you in Fern, Kestrel or Otter. You don't choose. Voice time, chatting, squads and the daily word all score for your house, and Monday's post says which one won the week.

  • Play on Monkey SMP

    Monkey SMP is the owner's modded Minecraft server. It takes its own pass. How to get in

Smaller things, if you want them: levels for chat and voice time (/rank), coins and a /shop for name colours, a daily word puzzle (/word guess), and a monthly bracket once four people sign up.

  3. Thu

    Free games

    Game night

        House rules

        The same rules are pinned in #rules on Discord. Monkey SMP has its own, on its site.

        1. Be decent. Trash-talk the play, not the person. No racism, sexism or slurs.
        2. No cheats, no exploits, no selling accounts.
        3. No spam or self-promo, and no DM advertising to members.
        4. Clips in #clips, memes in #memes, music in #bot-commands.
        5. Spoilers go under spoiler tags. No NSFW anywhere.
        6. Want a squad? Post in #lfg and ping @LFG, not @everyone.
        7. Something wrong? Right-click the message → Apps → Report message, or use /report.
        8. Mods have the final word.

        Questions

        Do I have to be good at games?

        You don't. Most of what we play is co-op against the game, so a bad run is everyone's bad run. Nobody tracks your K/D.

        How busy is it?

        It's a small server. Most of us are in US Pacific and play in the evening: some nights a few people are in voice, some nights nobody is. When the bot has fresh numbers, the Right now section of this page shows them.

        What happens when I join?

        Discord asks you to apply, and a mod approves it. Then you pick your games and land in the main channels. Say hi in general, and look at who is in voice.

        Is voice chat required?

        It isn't. Most of the talking happens in voice, so the text channels are on the quiet side. You're welcome to join a room muted and just listen.

        How do I get on the Minecraft server?

        Monkey SMP takes a pass. Season dates and how to get one are on its page. In the Discord, news goes in #monkey-smp, and the SMP Ping role tells you when three or more people are on.

        Why does Discord call it Lorenzo's Server?

        That's the server's name on Discord. Chill Gamers is what we call it here. It's the same place.

        What does the bot keep about me?

        Counts, not content. Front Desk notes how many messages you send each day, never what they say, plus your time in voice and the game Discord shows you playing. That drives levels, coins and house points. /privacy tracking:off stops it and deletes that history. The full list is in the privacy policy.

        My game isn't listed. Can I still join?

        Yes. Ask in general who else plays it. If a few people do, it can get a channel and a role.
