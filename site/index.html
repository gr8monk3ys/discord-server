What's in the server

Play together

  • Squad up with /lfg

    Post what you're playing and how many you need. People hit Join, and New Squad gives you a voice room of your own.

  • Game nights

    Friday is game night. Schedule your own with /gamenight or a watch party with /watchparty; reminders go out 15 minutes before.

  • Monthly tournaments

    A 16-player bracket for whichever game got played most that month. Sign up with a button and play Saturday night.

  • Houses

    You get sorted into Fern, Kestrel or Otter. What you do during the week scores for your house, and the House Cup goes out on Mondays.

Level up

  • Levels and rank cards

    XP for chatting and for time in voice. The roles run from Regular up to Mythic, and /rank shows your card.

  • Weekly challenges

    Three goals every Monday. Each pays coins, and clearing all three pays a bonus.

  • Coins, shop and seasons

    Coins come from hanging out. Spend them in the /shop on colours and perks. There are 23 badges to collect.

  • Starter quest

    /quest walks you through picking roles, saying hi and joining a first squad. It pays 500 coins.

Hang out

  • Clips and hall of fame

    Post your best plays. Members vote, and the winner is Clip of the Week.

  • Daily Word

    One five-letter word a day with /word guess. Results are spoiler-free, and streaks count.

  • Guides and setups

    Two forums: one for tips and builds, one for desks, rigs and bases. A post that stays up a day earns coins.

  • Creator spotlight

    Link your channel and your YouTube uploads and Twitch streams get posted in the creators channel.

        House rules

        1. 18+ only. If you're under 18, this isn't the server for you.
        2. Be decent. Trash-talk the play, not the person. Racism, sexism and slurs get you removed.
        3. Play fair. Cheats, exploits and account selling aren't welcome.
        4. Keep the ads out. That covers spam, self-promo and advertising in members' DMs.
        5. Keep it safe for work. Spoilers go under spoiler tags.
        6. Need a squad? Use /lfg and the game pings, not @everyone.
        7. Mods have the final word. Report anything off with /report.

        Questions

        Do I have to be good at games?

        You don't. Most of us are average on a good day, and nobody tracks your K/D.

        What happens when I join?

        A quick check to keep bots out, then you pick your games and land in general. /quest shows you around and pays 500 coins.

        How do I get roles?

        Pick your games during onboarding, then use the button panel in the roles channel (or /roles) for platform, region, play time and pings. Level roles, Tournament Champ and Clip of the Week are earned, and colour roles are in the /shop.

        How do levels work?

        You earn XP for chatting (once a minute, so spamming doesn't help) and for time in voice. Level 5 makes you a Regular, then Veteran at 10, Elite at 20, Legend at 30 and Mythic at 50. Check yours with /rank.

        Is voice chat required?

        It isn't. Plenty of people only text. Voice is there when you want it, and you can make your own room from the lobby.

        What time zone is everyone in?

        Scheduled events run on US Pacific time, but people play at all hours.

        My game isn't listed. Can I still join?

        Yes. Post it in the suggestions forum. If enough people play it, it gets a channel and a role.

        Can my server partner with yours?

        If it's a friendly community, probably. Join, run /partner apply, and staff will look at it. Approved partners are listed in the partners channel. Invites get checked weekly, so use a permanent one.
