Your first week

Four things a new member ends up doing. None of them is required.

  • Hop in voice

    The Lobby is the open voice room. If someone is in it, join them: that is how most games here start. For a room of your own, join New Squad and Front Desk makes one named after your game.

  • Find people for a game

    Post with /lfg: what you're playing, when, and how many you need. Members who picked that game get the ping. On a server this size, asking in general works too.

  • Get sorted into a house

    Front Desk, the server's bot, puts you in Fern, Kestrel or Otter. You don't choose. Time spent in voice and chat scores for your house, and Monday's post says which one won the week.

  • Play on The Realms

    The Realms is the owner's modded Minecraft server. Once you've been in the Discord for two weeks, /realms link with your Minecraft name puts you on its whitelist.

  • Levels for chatting and voice time. /rank shows your card.

A normal week

Times are US Pacific. Only the first row is people playing. The rest is Front Desk posting on a timer.

  1. Evenings

    Voice

    Whoever is around joins the Lobby and picks a game. Nothing is scheduled, and some nights nobody is on.

  2. Mon

    House result

    Front Desk adds up last week's house points and posts the winner. If nobody scored, it posts nothing.

  3. Thu

    Free games

    A list of PC games that are free to keep that week, when there are new ones.

  4. Fri

    Game night, if people come

    At noon Front Desk puts a 9 PM game night on the calendar for whatever was played most that week, unless a member has already set one up with /gamenight. It only happens if people turn up.

        House rules

        1. 18+ only. If you're under 18, this isn't the server for you.
        2. Be decent. Trash-talk the play, not the person. Racism, sexism and slurs get you removed.
        3. Play fair. Cheats, exploits and account selling aren't welcome.
        4. Keep the ads out. That covers spam, self-promo and advertising in members' DMs.
        5. Keep it safe for work. Spoilers go under spoiler tags.
        6. Need a squad? Use /lfg and the game pings, not @everyone.
        7. Mods have the final word. Report anything off with /report.

        Questions

        Do I have to be good at games?

        You don't. Most of what we play is co-op against the game, so a bad run is everyone's bad run. Nobody tracks your K/D.

        How busy is it?

        Not very. It's a small server, and on some nights nobody is on. When the bot has fresh numbers, the Right now section of this page shows them.

        What happens when I join?

        A quick check to keep bots out, then you pick your games and land in general. Say hi, and look at who is in voice.

        Is voice chat required?

        It isn't. Most of what happens here happens in voice though, so the text channels are quiet. You can join a room muted and listen.

        What time zone is everyone in?

        Mostly US Pacific, playing in the evening. Anything Front Desk schedules is set in Pacific time, and Discord shows events in your own time zone.

        How do I get on the Minecraft server?

        Join the Discord first. After two weeks, run /realms link with your Minecraft name and Front Desk adds you to the whitelist. The realms channel shows whether the server is up and who is on.

        My game isn't listed. Can I still join?

        Yes. Ask in general who else plays it. If a few people do, it can get a channel and a role.
