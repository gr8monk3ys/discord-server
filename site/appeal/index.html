Ask us to take another look.

If you were banned from the server, this form puts your side in front of the staff team. A person reads every appeal.

Before you start

  • You need your Discord user ID and your appeal code. Front Desk, the server's bot, sent both in a direct message when you were banned. Without a code this form can't reach anyone.
  • That message also gives the date appeals open for you, usually a week after the ban. Appeals sent earlier are discarded.
  • One appeal at a time. If it is denied you can send another after the waiting period in that message.
  • Plain words work best. Links aren't accepted.

Your appeal was received.

If the user ID and code belong together and appeals are open for you, it is now with the staff team. This page shows the same message either way, so it can't confirm that a code is right.

You don't need to send it again. Sending it twice doesn't speed anything up: extra copies are discarded.

What happens next

  • The staff team reads your answers and decides. That usually takes a few days and can take up to 30.
  • We often can't message you: Discord blocks the bot from messaging someone who is banned. If it can, Front Desk tells you the outcome. If you hear nothing after a week or two, try the invite link again. If it lets you in, the ban was lifted.

What is stored

  • Your user ID, your three answers and the time you sent them go to a private staff channel on the Discord server and are kept by the server's bot together with the decision, who made it and when.
  • The answers are erased from the bot's records 180 days after the decision. The record that an appeal was made stays, so the waiting period can be applied.
  • This page keeps nothing: it sets no cookies, has no trackers, and the form is not saved here. Our host records requests, including IP addresses, in its usual server logs.
